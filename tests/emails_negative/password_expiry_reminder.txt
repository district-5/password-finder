Action required: your network password expires in 5 days

Your network password will expire on Friday 4 September 2026.

To avoid being locked out, please change it before then. You can do this from
any company device by pressing Ctrl-Alt-Delete and choosing "Change a
password", or through the self-service portal at example.com/identity.

Your new password must:

  - be at least 14 characters long
  - contain upper case letters, lower case letters and a number
  - not repeat any of your previous 12 passwords
  - not contain your username or your name

If your password expires before you change it, you will need to contact the
service desk on extension 4400 to have it reset, and you will be asked to
confirm your identity before we can help.

Please note that we will never ask you for your password, whether by email, by
telephone or in person. If anyone asks you for it, report it to the security
team immediately.
